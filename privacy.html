OLYX privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026

OLYX is a habit tracker. It keeps as little about you as it can, shows no ads, uses no analytics and never sells or shares your data.

On your device

Your habits, ticks and settings are stored in your browser on your device. Without an account nothing leaves the device.

If you sign in with Google

  • We receive your Google account ID and email address from Google. We use them only to recognise your account. We get no access to your Google data (mail, contacts, files).
  • A copy of your habit data is stored on our server so it can sync between your devices.
  • Sign-in sessions are stored only as one-way hashes.

If you turn on notifications

On the web, we store your browser's push address, your time zone and an anonymous ID so we can send reminders and skip them once your day is done. Turning notifications off deletes this.

In the Android app, reminders are scheduled on your phone and nothing about them is stored on our server. When you are signed in, the app only tells the server that your day is complete, so your other devices skip the rest of that day's reminders.

Where

The server runs on Cloudflare (Workers, D1 and KV). Google provides sign-in.

Deleting your data

In OLYX go to Settings → Account → Delete my account data. This removes your habits, email and sessions from the server right away; your devices keep their local copy. Unused sessions also expire after a year.
